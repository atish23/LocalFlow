# Brag Plan: LocalFlow

## What is this app?
LocalFlow is a fast, 100% offline macOS voice dictation app powered directly by Apple Neural Engine and local Ollama, replacing cloud voice subscriptions like Wispr Flow with complete privacy and zero lag.

## The angle
Why pay $15/month to stream your confidential voice and code to cloud servers when Apple Silicon has a dedicated Neural Engine sitting idle in your lap? LocalFlow gives you push-to-talk dictation, real-time waveform feedback, and sub-500ms local LLM cleanup with zero cloud dependencies.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big, clean typographic punch: "Why is your voice dictation sending audio to the cloud?" A cloud icon crosses out into an Apple Silicon shield.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The Floating Pill HUD: Glowing macOS glass HUD with real-time reactive audio waveform bars and keycap shortcut indicators (Hold ⌃ to speak).
- The Local Pipeline: Apple Neural Engine STT + Local Ollama (Qwen 2.5 Coder) cleans speech and stutters in under 500ms.
- The Modern macOS App: Sleek Sonoma-style settings window with live 32-segment VU meter, custom developer vocabulary, and instant paste.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero subscriptions. Zero cloud lag. 100% on-device." LocalFlow logo, MIT open-source badge, and GitHub download link.

## User flow worth showing
Hold ⌃ (Push-to-Talk) -> Speak naturally with real-time waveform bars reacting -> Instant local LLM cleanup -> Clean text pastes directly into your active window.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: sleek Apple-style hardware-software launch film
- Interpretation: Confident, fast-paced, tactile, clean typography, dark glass aesthetics with vibrant orange/red waveform accents.
